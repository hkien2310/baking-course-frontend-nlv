# CMS Admin Dashboard Manual

Welcome to the Muka Baking Admin Dashboard. This guide will help you manage your website content, courses, and orders efficiently.

---

## 🚀 ACCESSING THE DASHBOARD
Visit `/login` and enter your Administrator credentials. Once authenticated, you will be redirected to `/admin`.

---

## 📋 CHAPTER 1: CONTENT MANAGEMENT

### 1.1 Programs (Courses)
Manage all your baking classes here.
- **General Info**: Edit title, price, sale price, and thumbnail.
- **Course Details**: Add learning goals, curriculum (modules), and "What's included" items.
- **Schedules**: Create "Class Sessions" (e.g., Monday 9AM-12PM). These sessions appear in the public timetable and checkout dropdown.
- **Cloudinary**: All images you upload are automatically hosted on Cloudinary for maximum speed and reliability.

### 1.2 Posts (Blog & Recipes)
- Write articles using the built-in editor.
- Assign categories to group related content.
- Featured images will be displayed on the blog listing and detail pages.

### 1.3 Categories
A dedicated section to manage categories for both **Programs** and **Posts**. 
- You can create, rename, or deactivate categories here.
- Changes reflect immediately in the public filtering menus.

---

## 💰 CHAPTER 2: ORDERS & PAYMENTS

### 2.1 Managing Orders
This is the heart of your business operations.
- **Order List**: View all transactions, customer names, and amounts.
- **Payment Methods**:
    - **VNPay**: These orders are automatically confirmed by the system once payment is successful.
    - **Manual Bank Transfer**: These orders stay in `AWAITING_CONFIRM`. You must check your bank account and manually approve them.
- **Order Details**: Click on an order to see full details, including VNPay transaction IDs or uploaded receipt images for manual transfers.

### 2.2 Payment Configuration (Settings)
Configure how customers pay you.
- Update your bank account details (Name, Number, Holder).
- Upload your Bank QR code image.
- Set the default transfer content (e.g., "MUKA [OrderCode]").

---

## ⚙️ CHAPTER 3: SYSTEM SETTINGS

### 3.1 Home Slider
The homepage hero banner is now managed under **Settings** or a dedicated **Sliders** tab.
- Choose which programs to feature on the main banner.
- Upload custom high-resolution background images for the slider.

### 3.2 Site Configuration
Manage general site info like contact phone numbers, email addresses, and social media links in one place.

---

## 🎓 CHAPTER 4: STUDENT SHOWCASE
Manage the "Student Work" section.
- Review photos submitted by students.
- **Approve** to show them on the public gallery or **Reject** to hide them.

---

## 👥 CHAPTER 5: CRM
- **Contact Messages**: Read and delete messages sent from the public contact form.
- **Enrollments**: Legacy list of sign-ups (for non-payment courses if applicable).

***
*Note: Always remember to "Save" your changes before navigating away from an edit screen.*
